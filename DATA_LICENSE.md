# Data License & Source Provenance

## Underlying Public Datasets

The raw and normalized examination datasets included or referenced in this project originate from official public open data published by:

- **CERMAT** (*Centrum pro zjišťování výsledků vzdělávání* / Centre for Evaluation of Educational Achievement)
- **MŠMT** (*Ministerstvo školství, mládeže a tělovýchovy ČR* / Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports of the Czech Republic)

### Scope & Terms

- **Underlying exam data**: CERMAT / MŠMT, subject to Czech public sector open data terms (Act No. 106/1999 Coll., on Free Access to Information and relevant national open data directives). These datasets are freely reusable for informational, analytical, educational, and commercial purposes with proper attribution to the source publisher.
- **Project code & original analysis**: Authored by Petr Salomoun under the [MIT License with Attribution Requirement](LICENSE).

### Distinction of Ownership

No proprietary ownership is claimed over the raw examination figures published by CERMAT/MŠMT. Ownership and attribution rights apply to the original code, data extraction and normalization pipeline, cohort matching logic, statistical models, derived metrics (including synthetic intake selectivity proxies and value-added residuals), interactive visualization dashboards, and report narratives authored in this repository.

### Attribution Guidance for Downstream Users

When referencing or republishing findings, charts, or transformed data from this project:
1. Attribute the analytical pipeline, methodology, and visualizations to **Petr Salomoun** ([czech_high_school_stats](https://github.com/petr-salomoun/czech_high_school_stats)).
2. Attribute the underlying raw examination data to **CERMAT / MŠMT**.
